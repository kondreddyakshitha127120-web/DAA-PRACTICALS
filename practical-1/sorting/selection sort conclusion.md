• Divide-and-conquer sorting algorithms (Merge Sort and Quicksort) perform significantly faster on large datasets with an average time complexity of \(O(n \log n)\) compared to simple quadratic algorithms (Bubble, Selection, and Insertion Sort) which take \(O(n^2)\) time. Insertion sort remains efficient for small or nearly sorted arrays, while Merge Sort guarantees \(O(n \log n)\) in all cases at the cost of \(O(n)\) extra space.
